
# Meridian Commercial Group (MCG)
## Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Dashboard | Power BI

## 1. Project Overview

The Meridian Commercial Group (MCG) Sales Performance & Business Intelligence project is an interactive business intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The project focuses on analyzing the company's sales performance through multiple business dimensions, including revenue generation, order activity, product categories, regional performance, salesperson contributions, and monthly revenue trends.

The dashboard transforms sales records into an organized visual reporting environment, allowing users to examine business performance, identify revenue contributors, compare sales activities, and monitor changes in revenue throughout the year.

Rather than presenting sales figures in isolation, the project brings together different aspects of sales performance to provide a clearer understanding of how products, geographical regions, salespeople, and time contribute to the company's overall revenue.

## 2. Project Objectives

The primary objective is to develop an interactive sales performance dashboard that supports business monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

The specific objectives are to:

- Evaluate the company's overall sales performance.
- Monitor total orders, total revenue, and average order value.
- Identify product categories contributing significantly to revenue.
- Compare revenue generated by individual products.
- Examine revenue distribution across geographical regions.
- Evaluate individual salesperson contributions.
- Analyze monthly revenue patterns and fluctuations.
- Provide interactive filtering for more focused business analysis.
- Present business information through an organized and accessible reporting interface.

## 3. Business Questions

The analysis was structured around the following business questions:

### Overall Sales Performance
1. How many orders were recorded?
2. What is the total revenue generated?
3. What is the average order value?
4. What does the relationship between total orders and revenue reveal about sales performance?

### Product Performance
5. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
6. Which individual products contribute most to total revenue?
7. How does revenue vary across the different product categories?

### Regional Performance
8. Which geographical region generates the highest revenue?
9. How is revenue distributed across the four regions?
10. Are there noticeable differences in regional sales contributions?

### Salesperson Performance
11. Who are the highest revenue-generating salespeople?
12. How does individual salesperson performance compare?
13. Which salespeople contribute the least to overall revenue?

### Monthly Revenue Performance
14. Which months record the highest revenue?
15. Which months experience lower revenue?
16. What patterns or fluctuations can be observed throughout the year?

## 6. Key Findings and Business Observations

Based on the figures displayed in the dashboard, the following observations can be made.

### Overall Performance
- The business recorded 369 orders.
- Total revenue reached approximately 435.07K.
- Average order value stood at approximately 1.18K.

### Product Performance
- Coffee generated the highest displayed product revenue, at approximately 75K.
- Curry Sauce followed with approximately 69K.
- Marmalade contributed approximately 41K.
- Mozzarella generated approximately 33K.

These results indicate that Coffee and Curry Sauce are the leading products among those displayed.

### Regional Performance
- North recorded the highest regional revenue at approximately 141.68K.
- East generated approximately 108.28K.
- South contributed approximately 93.86K.
- West generated approximately 91.25K.

North therefore represents the largest regional revenue contribution in the dashboard.

### Salesperson Performance
- Nancy Freehafer recorded the highest displayed salesperson revenue at approximately 104K.
- Anne Larsen followed with approximately 94K.
- Andrew Cencini generated approximately 67K.
- Jan Kotas recorded the lowest displayed contribution at approximately 16K.

The comparison highlights differences in individual revenue contributions.

### Monthly Revenue Performance

The monthly revenue trend shows noticeable fluctuations throughout the year.

- December recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately 67K.
- June generated approximately 56K.
- October recorded approximately 53K.
- February recorded the lowest monthly revenue at approximately 20K.

These observations highlight variations in monthly revenue performance and provide areas for further business investigation.

*Note: Figures are rounded as displayed in the dashboard.*

## 10. Business Value and Recommendations

The dashboard provides a foundation for several potential business decisions.

**Product Strategy**

Management can investigate the factors contributing to the strong revenue performance of Coffee and Curry Sauce and assess opportunities to improve the performance of other products.

**Regional Strategy**

The North region's leading revenue contribution can be examined to understand the factors behind its performance and identify opportunities in other regions.

**Sales Team Evaluation**

Salesperson revenue comparisons can support performance reviews, coaching, and further investigation into differences in individual contributions.

**Revenue Monitoring**

Monthly revenue patterns can help management identify periods requiring additional attention and support future sales planning.

These are potential areas for further investigation rather than conclusions about the causes of the observed performance.

## 11. Project Limitations and Further Analysis

The dashboard provides a sales performance overview, but additional information would be required to investigate some business questions in greater depth.

Possible future improvements include:

- Profitability and profit margin analysis.
- Year-over-year revenue comparison.
- Customer segmentation and purchasing behavior.
- Order volume by product and region.
- Sales target versus actual performance.
- Identification of seasonal sales patterns.
- Forecasting future revenue.
- Analysis of discounts and their effect on revenue.

These additions could expand the dashboard from sales performance reporting into a more comprehensive business intelligence solution.

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

The **Meridian Commercial Group (MCG) Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The project focuses on evaluating business sales performance through revenue generation, order activity, product contributions, geographical distribution, salesperson performance, and monthly revenue trends.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of the company's sales activities, helping users understand how different products, regions, and salespeople contribute to overall revenue.

By bringing these analytical dimensions together, the project demonstrates how business data can be transformed into meaningful visual insights that support performance monitoring and informed decision-making.

## PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The primary objective of this project is to develop an interactive reporting solution for monitoring and evaluating sales performance.

The specific objectives include:

- Evaluate overall business sales performance.
- Monitor total orders, revenue, and average order value.
- Identify high-performing product categories and individual products.
- Analyze revenue distribution across geographical regions.
- Evaluate individual salesperson contributions.
- Examine monthly revenue patterns and fluctuations.
- Provide interactive filters for focused business analysis.
- Present relevant business information through a consolidated dashboard.

---

## BUSINESS QUESTIONS

The analysis addresses the following business questions:

**Overall Sales Performance**
1. How many orders were recorded?
2. What is the total revenue generated?
3. What is the average order value?

**Product Performance**
4. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
5. Which individual products contribute most to revenue?
6. How do revenue contributions differ across products?

**Regional Performance**
7. Which geographical region generates the highest revenue?
8. How is revenue distributed across the four regions?
9. Which regions contribute less to overall revenue?

**Salesperson Performance**
10. Who are the highest revenue-generating salespeople?
11. How does individual salesperson performance compare?
12. Which salespeople record the lowest revenue contributions?

**Monthly Revenue Trends**
13. Which months generate the highest revenue?
14. Which months experience lower revenue?
15. What fluctuations can be observed throughout the year?

## KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)

The dashboard presents three major performance indicators.

| KPI | Result |
|:---|---:|
| Total Orders | 369 |
| Total Revenue | 435.07K |
| Average Order Value | 1.18K |

### KPI Interpretation

**Total Orders**

Represents the number of orders recorded in the dataset, providing an overview of business order activity.

**Total Revenue**

Represents the overall revenue generated from the recorded sales transactions.

**Average Order Value**

Measures the average revenue generated per order, providing an additional perspective on the value of individual transactions.

## BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Based on the observed sales performance, the following areas could support further business investigation.

**1. Product Strategy**

Investigate the factors behind the strong revenue contributions of Coffee and Curry Sauce and identify opportunities to improve the performance of other products.

**2. Regional Performance**

Examine the factors contributing to the North region's leading performance and explore opportunities to strengthen sales in other regions.

**3. Salesperson Performance**

Use individual revenue comparisons to support performance reviews, coaching, and further investigation into differences in sales contributions.

**4. Revenue Monitoring**

Monitor monthly revenue fluctuations to identify periods that may require additional sales attention and planning.

These recommendations represent potential business actions for further investigation, rather than established explanations for the observed results.
